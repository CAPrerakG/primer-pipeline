## What this industry actually is

The useful organising question is: **does the customer pay to acquire a machine, use a machine, or replace something that wears inside a machine?** Those are different businesses even when every invoice mentions construction or mining.

ACE and Ajax sell machines that enable customers to complete construction work. Eimco sells mining machinery and spares. Gujarat Apollo combines an equipment operation with substantial investment and associate economics. Tega is chiefly a mining consumables business, now materially transformed by the Molycop acquisition, with a separate equipment operation. Sanghvi earns rental and project-service income from owning and deploying cranes. Cranex makes industrial overhead cranes and installs escalators. TIL manufactures material-handling equipment but added a gas-compression subsidiary during the latest quarter. Virat Crane is a dairy company. Treating these as nine interchangeable OEMs would corrupt both the commercial explanation and the historical basket.

The listed roster is therefore a sampling frame to be audited, not a definition of the Indian equipment market. A complete primer must separately describe large unlisted Indian operations of global OEMs, excavator/backhoe-loader competition, and dealer/financier economics. It must not imply that a five-company provisional basket captures the industry's national market share or demand mix.

The dated industry baseline is useful for scale, not as a current FY27 forecast. ICEMA's 28 May 2025 release reports FY25 total sales of 140,191 units, up 3%, including domestic sales 126,961 and exports 13,230; earthmoving represented 71% of units. These figures describe the association's equipment categories and unit basis, not this listed roster's revenue pool. Read the original three-page release before final citation; newer FY26 evidence still needs to be obtained for the full primer. [ICEMA FY25 release](https://www.i-cema.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/ICEMA-Press-Release_Annual-Sales-Report-FY25_28 May 25.pdf)

## Commercial thesis to develop

**Productivity must become customer cash before it becomes supplier pricing power.** A contractor buys a machine when its expected output, utilisation, financing cost and resale value justify the investment. Government capex announcements alone do not establish that bridge. Project awards must turn into site mobilisation, contractor liquidity, equipment orders and deliveries. Equipment makers can report sales growth while dealers or customers absorb inventory. A rental operator can report high utilisation while working capital or new fleet purchases consume cash. A mining consumable supplier can generate replacement demand from an existing mine even when the mine orders no new equipment.

The full primer should distinguish equipment purchase from replacement cycles. A crane, loader or concrete mixer is long-lived and can be resold; a wear liner is consumed. A machine's installed base creates a potential market for parts and service, but those economics may accrue to the dealer, independent repairer, component supplier or original manufacturer. Evidence should identify who owns the customer relationship, who has replacement specifications, which parts are interchangeable, and whether service revenue is separately disclosed.

Mining equipment and mining consumables also respond to different operating decisions. New underground equipment can depend on mine-development approvals and customer procurement. Liners and grinding media depend on ore throughput, hardness, wear life and processing conditions. Tega's acquisition expands the latter exposure, making an undifferentiated construction-capex explanation particularly misleading. Contract duration, FX, acquired financing and purchase-price allocation now matter alongside volume growth.

For a machine OEM, low weight or low tonnage does not automatically mean low value. Hydraulic design, controls, machining, performance testing, reliability and distribution can matter more than steel tonnage. For a rental business, owning a heavier crane can increase both achievable rental income and idle-capital risk. The relevant comparison is contribution after depreciation, mobilisation, maintenance and interest, followed by cash after replacement capex.

## Accounting work completed

All nine companies have current-quarter and same-quarter-prior-year primary revenue, ordinary other income, reportedPAT, owner-profit treatment andEPS recorded. All nine annual basicEPS figures reconcile with independently disclosed weighted share counts within the published rounding precision. This does **not** certify every quarterly denominator or the underlying accounts.

All five provisional basket candidates—ACE, Eimco, Gujarat Apollo, Tega and TIL—have a primary four-quarterFY26 bridge for revenue, other income and the relevant reported profit line. Those quarterly totals reconcile to the original annual statements. The ledger preserves the distinction between this arithmetic check and the EPS/shareholder check. For TIL, the September locator returns a one-page explanatory filing; the September values used in the bridge are the comparative quarter in the December primary results.

Gujarat Apollo needs particular care. The original March statement'sPAT line is 515.64 lakh; the later June comparative prints 515.65 lakh. Adding annual associate profit 79.43 lakh gives 595.07 lakh from the former, while the annualEPS note has 595.08 lakh. The 1,000 rupee difference is retained. The June-quarter PAT line 97.49 lakh less associate loss 16.81 lakh gives the 80.68 lakh earnings numerator; it must not be confused with comprehensive income 134.80 lakh. The prior-year quarterlyEPS0.98 is marginally outside simple fixed-share rounding tolerance and remains flagged.

ACE's annual owner numerator is 415.0905 cr, versus the quarter-result rounded group 415.10 cr. The exact annual weighted basic shares are 119,011,646 after treasury-share adjustment. That annual count is not automatically the correctJune-quarter count. The current quarterly denominator remains open even though the profit scale is plausible.

Tega's acquisition makes owner attribution essential. The latestEPS follows owner loss, not group loss. Its historicalFY26 quarterlyEPS checks also reconcile: the December denominator changes because 8,592,206 shares were issued on 28 November 2025. The date-weighted quarter calculation is retained, separately from the annual note's weighted count. Reported revenue growth across the acquisition boundary is not organic growth.

A suspected TIL capital discrepancy was **resolved rather than published as an error**. The rights issue comprised 12,091,760 partly-paid shares withRs 7.50 paid face value, explaining the 906.882 lakh paid-capital increase. The registrar's application form confirms the 75%payment structure. Remaining work is the correct quarterly ownerEPS numerator and weighted entitlement allocation, not a claim that capital itself is wrong. Original annualEPS−4.58 reconciles with 67,239,042 weighted shares; the June filing's annual comparator−4.53 is on a rights-adjusted basis. [Primary rights application](https://web.in.mpms.mufg.com/RightsOffers/PDF/TIL%20 Limited%20-%20 Rights%20 Issue%202026_Application_Form.pdf)

Cranex's annual basicEPS arithmetic passes, but its diluted note uses a larger denominator while also saying there are no dilutive instruments. That is a disclosure contradiction, separate from the current-quarter weighted-share question and the auditor's qualification. Do not let a passed EPS multiplication imply clean accounts.

## Price audit and prospective basket

All nine pilot-only series end exactly 18 September 2026. No later bar enters the audit. Ajax has 392 observations and Tega 1184; do not describe either as having a fifteen-year individual history. No large daily move flag appears for the five proposed members. Eimco has a 91-daygap in 2006 and TIL a 91-daygap in 2007–08, both before a 2012 backtest. Virat's−50.42%day is in 2010 and its exclusion is business-based.

The provisional broad basket is ACE/Eimco/GujaratApollo/Tega/TIL with changing membership when Tega lists. This is a proposal, not an implemented roster edit. A narrow equipment comparison should omitTega; an investment-income sensitivity should omitGujaratApollo; a restructuring sensitivity should omitTIL. Ajax should remain visible in a current-operating-company comparison despite exclusion from the seasonal base.

The daily jump/gap scan is a screening check, not a corporate-action certificate. Split-adjusted TradingView closes do not by themselves establish a shareholder total-return series. No price repair has been applied. No basket statistics, seasonality claims, rankings or historical performance narrative were fabricated for this pilot.

## Feasibility judgment

A reusable source pack and serious financial review are feasible in parallel folders without touching production state. The main constraint is review depth: current-perimeter acquisitions, scannedtables, treasury/partly-paid shares and profitbelowOCI require human-style reading and arithmetic. Downloading manyPDFs is substantially easier than completing the audit. This pilot supports measured research throughput, not a forecast that 223 of 278 sections can be fully drafted, audited, visually checked and published by 4 October.
