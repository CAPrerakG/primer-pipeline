# Parallel research pilot: feasibility of 223 completed sections

For Prerak. Recorded 1 October 2026. Research and prices use the fixed 18 September 2026 cutoff.

## Decision

**Parallel research is useful, but this pilot does not support committing to 223 completed sections by 4 October with the remaining allowance and one manual reset, while preserving the required quality.** Usage is the clearest constraint. Research exceptions and the unmeasured writing/review stages add uncertainty.

The repository still has **90 published primers covering 100 completed sections**. None of 091–093 has been published or counted as complete. These are reusable research packages with explicit unfinished checks.

## What remains before these become primers

- Exact quarterly weighted share counts or owner-profit reconciliation in several companies. A closing share count is sometimes only an approximation.
- Resolution or explicit disclosure of contradictory primary filings. A contradiction is different from a check that has not been performed.
- Some excluded-company quarter/half-year bridges and current-period source gaps. PIGL's apparent current result proved to be a May annual-result filing; current June primary evidence remains unresolved.
- Deeper operating research: customers, orders, competition, margins, cash conversion, industry evidence and historical events.
- Price-history repairs and basket decisions, followed by statistics.
- The complete manuscript, source-linked tables, charts, glossary, analytical boxes, build, gate, verify, regression/browser checks and publication.

Consequently, **this was not a measurement of completed-primer throughput**. Calling it three completed primers would materially overstate progress.

## Important findings and corrections within the pilot

1. **Classification matters.** Virat Crane is a dairy business. Continental Controls reports advisory services. Current automation operations cannot automatically be projected backward through Axis's legacy listed-company history.
2. **Other income can dominate the apparent operating result.** International Conveyors requires a separate investment-exposure treatment. Aplab's ordinary other income includes a property-sale gain. These are not interchangeable with manufacturing growth.
3. **EPS needs the correct earnings and share denominator.** Owner profit, group profit, preference dividends, treasury shares, partly paid rights shares and acquisitions all created exceptions. TIL's partly paid shares explained one preliminary capital mismatch; that issue was corrected rather than retained as an accusation.
4. **A link label is not proof of period or content.** PIGL's quarter labels led to the same annual-result document. Universal's company-site annual-report file contained only a Not Found page. The latter was replaced with a real 79-page BSE filing dated 27 August 2026.
5. **Independent inspection remains necessary.** Visual checks corrected OCR readings and identified small source discrepancies. Universal's other-income quarter sum differs from the annual figure; the discrepancy remains visible.

The company ledgers contain source URLs, PDF pages, units, reporting basis and unresolved checks. No earlier published primer was changed during this pilot.

This is an account-wide, integer-rounded usage indicator, not exact token accounting for these workers. Concurrent activity could affect it. It is a warning about scale, not a precise cost forecast.

At the observed rate, 33 initial company reviews cost about 17 percentage points. A simple linear illustration for the other 1,963 entries is **about 1,011 percentage points**, before completing the remaining checks or writing/publishing the primers. The 112-point budget would fund roughly **217 more initial company reviews** at that rate, also before those additional stages. Sector differences and fixed setup costs will change the actual result; the observed shortfall is too large to dismiss as rounding.

The account currently reports a scheduled reset at **15:06 IST on 4 October**, less than nine hours before the assumed end-of-day deadline. The effect of using a manual reset on that schedule has not been established, so the base budget does not double-count it. Even a hypothetical extra full allowance would not close the illustrated research-only gap. Only the one manual reset Prerak authorized is assumed; no reset has been applied by the agent.

There were approximately 74.4 hours to the deadline at pilot start. Reaching 223 would require about **40 additional completed sections per day**. Existing recommended merges imply about **111 further primers**, before any additional justified merges or necessary splits: roughly **1.22–1.55 million words** at the required length. Nineteen target sections contain more than 30 names, including IT services with 128 and construction/EPC with 98. The 10/9/14-company pilot does not represent that larger tail.

Research wall-clock speed alone therefore cannot establish deadline feasibility. There is no measured end-to-end publication rate from this pilot and no sound basis for promising a specific alternative completion count yet.

## Recommended next step

1. **Finish 091 from the saved evidence and measure one complete publication.** Record additional usage, elapsed time, unresolved-check closure and all required quality gates. That supplies the missing cost of turning research into a published primer.
2. If Prerak approves scaling, use three research workers with separate company/file ownership and one integrating reviewer. Break large rosters into manageable company groups, then reunite the evidence before drafting. This speeds collection and review without counting partial work as completion.
3. Reuse downloaded sources, source-page ledgers, company classifications and price files. Add source-content/period validation at intake, because HTTP success and a PDF extension proved insufficient. Keep automation focused on repeatable calculations and rendering checks; it does not certify financial claims.
4. Reforecast after each small batch of actual publications. Keep the fixed data date, source thresholds, table/chart requirements, EPS checks and publication gates unchanged.
5. Treat 4 October as a progress checkpoint unless substantially more capacity is available. If 223 remains the required finished count, extend the schedule; if the date remains fixed, accept fewer fully completed sections. Do not meet the number by publishing incomplete work.

Fresh chats are authorized when context becomes difficult to manage or quality deteriorates. Each must start from the operating contract and a saved handoff containing sources, decisions, unresolved checks and tests. Any affected primer must be rechecked/redone. A fresh chat does not itself correct evidence or replenish allowance. No fresh chat was needed or created during this pilot.
