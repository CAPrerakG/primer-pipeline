Method. Returns are equal-weighted baskets of each section's listed members with sufficient history and scale, measured against the Nifty 50 on TradingView daily closes, with monthly returns capped at ±50%; 2026 figures run to 18 September. Company rosters are audit-corrected rather than raw watchlist membership, and every reclassification is stated on the page. Where a section has too few listed years to support a statistic, the primer says so instead of computing one.
